# Achievement lists

These achievements use explicit lists in `config/osiagniecia.csv`. Planned places
do not count. Removing a visit recalculates progress; developer unlocks remain
controlled by the existing developer settings.

## King of Kashubia

The eight counties are Bytów, Chojnice, Gdańsk, Kartuzy, Kościerzyna, Lębork,
Puck and Wejherowo, plus the county-level cities Gdańsk, Gdynia and Sopot.
Visiting the required county means marking its subregion on the map. This is a
county-based game objective; several counties extend beyond Kashubia.

Basis: the contemporary settlement area described in
[Granice Kaszub na przestrzeni wieków](https://kaszebsko.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Historio-Kaszebow-3.pdf).
The description quotes one line of the traditional folk song
[Kaszëbsczé Nótë](https://www.kartuzy.pl/strona/2007-kaszebscze-note-kaszubskie-nuty-abecadlo-kaszubskie).

## In Search of the Promised Land

The achievement uses the following **fixed interpretation for the game**, rather
than claiming a uniquely defined list of modern states in an ancient text.
Ancient regional names do not follow today's borders. References include the
Old and New Testaments and the deuterocanonical books. We do not expand an
empire's name to every modern country it once controlled.

English requirements. This is deliberately an explicit game list: it does not
resolve disputed ancient geography or include every possible interpretation.
